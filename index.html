How to play

Fight through a 10-round arena trial. Your starter is locked until the Pokémon roster opens in Round 5.

Choosing your Pokémon

A random Basic Pokémon becomes your starter and stays locked in through Round 4. The roster opens in Round 5: switch to another eligible Basic Pokémon, or keep building your starter along its own evolution line. Branching evolutions let you choose the final path. Check both fighters’ type icons before picking—use a type that is strong against the opponent whenever you can.

Battling turn by turn

Choose an attack, watch it resolve, then the CPU takes its turn. Regular attacks build energy; spend it on a gold special, evolution, a power form, Terastallization, or a Z-Move. Use your once-per-round Potion before HP gets too low. Save Mega Evolution, Dynamax, Terastallization, and Z-Moves for dangerous matchups or finishing blows. Ally Surge begins in Round 7 and boosts your whole team for the late-round bosses.

Attack and build energy

Tap one of four attacks each turn. Regular attacks build energy up to 5. Type advantage deals 35% extra damage. Gold special attacks cost 3 energy and never miss.

Recover and evolve

Your one Potion restores 25 HP each round. Evolution costs 2 energy, follows your Pokémon’s own evolution line, and resets energy to zero.

Power forms

Eligible Pokémon can Mega Evolve, Dynamax, Gigantamax, or Terastallize for 3 energy. Dynamax adds 60 HP and powers up attacks; Terastallization adds 25% HP and unlocks a free type-matched Tera Special.

Z-Moves

Each Pokémon can unleash one devastating Z-Move for 3 energy. It becomes free while that Pokémon is Terastallized, Dynamaxed, or Gigantamaxed.

Unlock stronger fighters

Legendary, Mythical, and Ultra Beast fighters unlock after 2 round wins. Their first power unlock costs 3 energy and later unlocks for that fighter cost 2.

Attack with rare Pokémon from Round 6

1Win at least 2 rounds, then build 3 energy with regular attacks.
2From Round 6, open the roster and choose a Legendary, Mythical, or Ultra Beast. Switching spends the 3 energy.
3Recharge, then use Power form, Terastallize, or a Z-Move. The first rare power costs 3 energy; later rare powers for that fighter cost 2.

After transforming, that rare Pokémon’s attacks become free until it returns to normal—use its gold special attack to press the advantage.

Survive the late rounds

Rounds 6–7 are very hard. From Round 7, Ally Surge gives your team 75% more HP and 1.75× attack. Rounds 8–10 are extreme; Round 9 is a Mythical Pokémon or Ultra Beast, and Round 10 is a Legendary or Mythical boss.

Win at least 8 of 10 rounds to become Arena Champion. You can afford up to 2 losses.

New player? Turn on Easy Mode above the arena to begin with a second-stage Pokémon, qualify with 5 wins, and learn with room for up to 5 losses.
